The Times Group’s rich legacy dates to 1838, when its flagship publication, The Times of India, was first published, making it the oldest English-language newspaper in India. Today, it is the largest-selling English daily in the world. In addition, the Times Group also publishes leading regional newspapers, including Navbharat Times (Hindi), Maharashtra Times (Marathi), Vijay Karnataka (Kannada), and Ei Samay Sangbadpatra (Bengali). The media properties of the Group also include: The Economic Times, Mumbai Mirror, Bangalore Mirror, Times Now, ET Now, Zoom, Radio Mirchi, and Femina Miss India.
