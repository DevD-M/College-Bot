Yes, Bennett University is UGC approved. The UGC website lists Bennett University among the 300+ private universities established by the Acts of Legislatures of various states, which are authorized to confer degrees as specified by the UGC under Section 22 of the UGC Act.
UGC is the apex body that governs all universities in India. As per law, universities do not require All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) approval to run technical programs. Only institutions affiliated to a university (and not universities themselves) need AICTE approval. Since Bennett University is a bonafide university (self-financed) established by the State Legislature of Uttar Pradesh, it is governed by the UGC and therefore does not require AICTE approval. As per the Supreme Court judgment (case 2056 of 1999) in Bharathidasan University and Others Vs. All India Council for Technical Education and Others (2001) 8 SCC 676, it is not mandatory for the State/Private Universities to take AICTE approval to run technical programmes.
