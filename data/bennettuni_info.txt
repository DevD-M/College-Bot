Bennett University is located in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India.
It was established in 2016 by the Times of India Group (Bennett, Coleman & Co. Ltd).

COURSES OFFERED:
- B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering (CSE)
- B.Tech CSE with specializations: AI/ML, Cybersecurity, Data Science, IoT
- B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- BBA, MBA, B.Com, M.Com
- BA Journalism and Mass Communication
- LLB, LLM

FEES:
- B.Tech: approximately 2.5 to 3 lakh per semester
- Scholarships available based on JEE Main ranks

CAMPUS FACILITIES:
- Wi-Fi enabled campus
- Hostels for boys and girls
- Sports complex, gym, swimming pool
- Library with digital resources
- Medical center on campus
- Cafeteria and food courts

PLACEMENTS:
- Average package: 6-8 LPA
- Top recruiters: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Amazon, Microsoft, Deloitte
- Highest package: 40+ LPA
