The Central Library at Bennett University is a one-stop place in meeting the information needs of Bennett fraternity to include students, research scholar’s faculty and staff. The Library is housed in a state-of-the-art infrastructure and equipped with latest to complement the requirements for academics and research. The library is well stocked with relevant and appropriate learning and information resources both in print and electronic, with the latest collection of books, journals, magazines, e-journals, eBooks and databases from globally known publishers. The major focus of the library is online/digital learning resources which allows users to access them anytime, anywhere 24×7.
The Library is fully automated using KOHA – Integrated Library Management System with 3M electromagnetic/RFID enabled security gates, smart card, self-check in and checkout, RFID drop box etc. Bennett University has launched its Mobile App with variety of features and services. The Mobile OPAC (M-OPAC) is one of the imperative features of University Mobile App. Students not only can search books through the M-OPAC, they can also renew books, reserve books, create virtual shelve for their reading and suggest book purchase for the library.
Taking a cue from academic partner, Georgia Tech USA, the library has planned to develop Intellectual Repository of Bennett University in the name of “BU-DRS” using D space-An Open Source Software. The purpose of the BU-DRS is to capture, collect, organize, preserve and disseminate intellectual output of the Bennett University locally and globally. Many more exciting initiatives are being planned in the near future to make the library as the hub of academic and learning activities.
The Law Library undeniably holds a prominent space in the School of Law, Bennett university. Law library measuring 10, 000 sq.ft is in tower A of the Bennett University. The School of Law is in process of developing a world-class law library with subjects covering various fields of study ranging from law, management to social sciences. The Library is fully automated using KOHA – Integrated Library Management System and Online and Mobile Public Access Catalogue (M/OPAC) which will enable users to search library’s collection and guides them to precise locations. The School of law is also in a process of building a E-Library wherein user can access the wide range of database from outside the campus. At present School of Law has subscribed to online databases like Manupatra.com, Supreme Court Case Online (SCCONLINE), All India Reporter (AIR Online) and HEINONLINE..
The library is currently in the process of creating a database of wide-ranging national and international journals and e-resources like JSTORE, WESTLAW, KUWERONLINE, etc.
Working hours of Library are 9 AM to 12 Midnight on weekdays; 9 AM to 5 PM on Sunday and other Holidays.
