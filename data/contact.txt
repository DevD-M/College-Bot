School of Computer Science and Engineering, Bennett University
The BEST Journal aims to be a beacon of student research, providing a professional outlet for their research findings and fostering a culture of scholarly communication and intellectual growth. BEST Journal aspires to be a lighthouse in the realm of student research, illuminating the path of innovation and discovery at Bennett University. We aim to mold a generation of scholars and pioneers, driving forward the frontiers of knowledge in our fields.
Our mission is two-fold: to foster a culture of rigorous academic inquiry among students and to provide a revered platform for the publication and dissemination of their research. We strive to bridge the gap between academic learning and real-world application, enhancing the visibility and impact of our students’ work on a global stage.
