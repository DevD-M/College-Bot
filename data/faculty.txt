Students interested in pursuing law as a career and the parents of these prospective law students start with the basic query of “Why choose law as a career?” In the process of admission at the law school, there are deliberations on the process of selection at school committees, allocation of sections, mess, or hostel policy documents. While indirectly, students and their parents engage in discussions of fairness, transparency and justice which forms a part of study of law. Thus, while one studies law as a professional course, one experiences law regularly in their daily lives.
The relevance of law in today’s uncertain times cannot be stressed enough. The study as well as the practice of law is enriching, exciting and of immense social relevance. Legal education not only richly enhances prospects of a bright career but also promotes essential skills such as accuracy of expression, facility in argument and interpretation of the written and spoken word. Law, being the foundation of the society, helps one understand the social sentiments, values, and aspirations. Those equipped with the knowledge of law become responsible agents of political and social change, transformation, and reform. Attaining legal education, therefore, is also about inventing oneself. The journey is transformative. The quest of their sense of ‘justice’ and ‘achievement’ takes precedence over ‘financial returns’. If the student has this quest in them, they would be successful in the field of law.
Indeed, School of Law, Bennett University believes in creating not just another set of law graduates, but budding lawyers who passionately deliver justice with a firm foundation in ethics along with thorough professional acumen. The School of Law is established with the vision of educating and training thought leaders in the field of law. The students imbibe the best practices of national and international law schools in terms of curriculum, pedagogy, innovation in teaching methods along with the know-how to address emerging contemporary issues. Bennett University, being an educational undertaking of the Times of India Group, naturally gains from the robust tradition of this media house in voicing the rights of people and the true interests of the nation.
In the era marked by rapid economic growth, technological advancements, and evolving global affairs, the role of legal education stands at the forefront. The swift transitions not only present challenges but also create opportunities for continuous learning and adaptation, shaping legal professionals capable of meeting contemporary demands.
At Bennett School of Law, our mission is to nurture future-ready professionals by providing robust exposure to interdisciplinary education, project-based learning, clinical legal education, and critical thinking, all while striving for excellence. Our School has made significant strides through experiential learning opportunities such as legal aid, mooting, internships, debating, and more. These platforms immerse students in real-world legal challenges, allowing them to refine their advocacy skills while upholding the values of empathy, justice, and service.
Backed by the 187-year-old Times Group legacy, Bennett University, with NAAC A+ accreditation, remains committed to academic excellence, innovation, and research, continuing its mission to shape socially conscious changemakers. The School of Law promotes a robust research culture through its twenty specialised research cells addressing contemporary legal and societal issues and engaging faculty and students in legal and policy research. There is a robust faculty mentoring system in place ensuring a smooth and enriching transition from school to university and thereafter to the professional world. Thank you for choosing Bennett University in your pursuit of higher education with a purpose of positive social impact. Believe in yourself and remain a lifelong learner.
